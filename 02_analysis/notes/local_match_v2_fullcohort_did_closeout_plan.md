## Parallel stayer-worktree boundary

After this implementation plan is accepted, a separate chat may create
`.worktrees/lmv2-p5b-stayers` and begin the initially-retained-inventor
definition, roster, balance, and selection work in parallel. It must not edit
the current P6 full-cohort worktree or reuse full-cohort weights.

The stayer worktree may use the already frozen patent-count and
active-patenting definitions. It should not freeze or interpret citation
outcomes until D5 completes the Cassi--Ornaghi citation-source audit. Final
integration occurs only after both worktrees provide certified manifests,
explicit sample/estimand definitions, and reproducible output packages. This
keeps the stayer roster, weights, selection diagnostics, and bounds separate
while allowing the computational work to proceed in parallel.

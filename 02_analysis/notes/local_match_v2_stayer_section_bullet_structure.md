# Stayer section: bullet-point writing structure

Target length: 6.0--7.5 pages, excluding appendix

## 6.1 Population, definition, and selection (0.75--1.0 page)

- Define `initially retained`:
  - pre-deal target inventor;
  - first observed patent in \(t=+1,\ldots,+5\) is linked to the focal merged
    group;
  - \(t=0\) never determines retention.
- State what the definition does and does not measure:
  - patent affiliation, not employment;
  - selected post-treatment status;
  - not an always-stayer principal stratum.
- Report the certified partition:
  - 28,483 total pre-deal target inventors;
  - 3,090 initially retained;
  - 1,158 leavers;
  - 24,235 with no observed post-deal patent.
- Present pre-deal selection table:
  - five-year patent stock: 3.78 retained, 2.85 leaver, 1.73 no-post;
  - active baseline years: 1.93, 1.64, 1.24;
  - career age: 3.88, 3.92, 2.97.
- Interpretation bullets:
  - retained inventors are positively selected on recent patenting relative
    to leavers;
  - retained and leaver career age is effectively identical;
  - management-transition explanation for the no-post group is not supported
    by the frozen career-age diagnostic;
  - raw retained-leaver sorting does not sign the bias of the separately
    balanced retained-control contrast.
- Main exhibit:
  - one compact selection table.

## 6.2 Patent output of initially retained inventors (1.25--1.5 pages)

- Design support:
  - 2,663 of 3,090 initially retained inventors matched: 86.2%;
  - 151 nominal treated deals;
  - 31.5 effective treated deals;
  - essentially exact post-weighting balance.
- Primary result---keep in full:
  - \(\mathrm{ATT}=-0.107\);
  - 95% CI \([-0.204,-0.010]\);
  - \(p=0.031\);
  - five-year cumulative contrast: \(-0.536\) patents.
- Economic magnitude:
  - observed post mean: 0.816;
  - matched counterfactual: 0.923;
  - 11.6% decline relative to the counterfactual;
  - 14.2% decline relative to pre-deal output.
- Inference qualification:
  - primary two-way deal/inventor inference;
  - deal-wild interval \([-0.224,0.015]\), \(p=0.081\), as conservative
    sensitivity because controls recur across deal donor pools.
- Sign stability:
  - buffered 1994--2008 estimate: \(-0.120\);
  - full-sample ATT-to-largest-held-out-gap ratio: 0.95;
  - buffered ratio: 1.41;
  - held-out pre-period gaps prevent an unqualified always-stayer causal
    claim.
- Main exhibit:
  - retained-inventor patent-path/event-study figure.

## 6.3 Quantity anatomy, quality, and technological direction (1.0--1.25 pages)

- P8 retained-inventor accounting decomposition:
  - end of observed patenting: 27.3% of the total decline;
  - lower activity conditional on remaining observable: 5.4%;
  - fewer patents in active inventor-years: 67.3%;
  - interpretation: most of the retained contrast occurs while inventors
    continue patenting.
- Guardrail:
  - accounting decomposition, not causal mediation;
  - active patenting is a post-treatment outcome.
- OECD PQII:
  - linkage-scaled total: \(-0.043\);
  - 95% CI \([-0.094,0.009]\);
  - \(p=0.102\);
  - conditional PQII per patent is close to zero;
  - interpretation: total quality-adjusted output may fall with quantity, but
    surviving patents are not shown to become worse.
- TechDrift:
  - estimate: \(+0.009\);
  - 95% CI \([-0.014,0.032]\);
  - \(p=0.432\);
  - no detectable broad movement away from pre-deal IPC portfolios.
- Citation evidence:
  - positive descriptive contrasts;
  - unfavorable pretrends;
  - no causal quality-upgrade claim.
- Mechanism interpretation:
  - pattern is consistent with selective project pruning;
  - pattern does not prove portfolio rationalization.

## 6.4 Durability of initial retention (1.0--1.25 pages)

- Full initially retained population:
  - focal-only patent affiliation: 51.3% at \(+1\), 19.1% at \(+5\);
  - endpoint focal persistence: 32.7% through \(+3\), 20.9% through \(+5\);
  - uninterrupted annual focal evidence: 10.0% through \(+3\), 3.1% through
    \(+5\).
- \(+5\) state composition:
  - 19.1% focal only;
  - 2.3% focal and outside;
  - 3.8% outside without focal;
  - 17.5% no patent that year but later patent exists;
  - 53.5% end of observed patenting;
  - 3.9% right-censored.
- Symmetric matched descriptive comparison:
  - endpoint persistence through \(+3\): 31.4% treated, 36.4% control;
  - endpoint persistence through \(+5\): 20.2%, 24.2%;
  - uninterrupted through \(+5\): 2.6%, 4.7%.
- Required adjacent caveat:
  - controls come from firms required to remain patent-active through
    \(g+5\);
  - treated focal groups have no symmetric existence requirement;
  - higher control persistence is partly definitional;
  - descriptive only, no inference.
- Main exhibit:
  - persistence figure with endpoint and uninterrupted definitions.

## 6.5 Collaboration networks and team recomposition (1.5--2.0 pages)

- Begin with the credibility boundary:
  - strict full-cohort causal network design was prospectively frozen;
  - persistent tie: at least two joint applications in at least two years
    during \(t=-5,-4,-3\);
  - strict design covers only 8.2% of weighted full-cohort treated mass;
  - composition validation rows: 745 at \(-2\), 557 at \(-1\), below 1,000;
  - Path Q: no causal post-acquisition network ATT.
- Explain why the retained descriptive analysis is still informative:
  - different population: all 2,663 matched initially retained inventors;
  - treated-only, exploratory, post-on-post description;
  - does not reopen Path Q.
- Define four mutually exclusive collaborator categories:
  - legacy target colleague;
  - legacy acquirer colleague;
  - collaborator new to both pre-deal groups;
  - outside-focal-group collaborator.
- Pooled \(+1\)-through-\(+5\) composition:
  - 34.6% legacy target;
  - 3.4% legacy acquirer;
  - 51.3% new to both groups;
  - 10.7% outside focal group.
- Dynamic team recomposition:
  - legacy target share: 49.5% at \(+1\) to 19.7% at \(+5\);
  - legacy acquirer share: 3.4% to 3.6%, essentially flat;
  - new-to-both share: 43.8% to 55.6%;
  - outside-group share: 3.2% to 21.1%.
- Direct baseline-tie evidence:
  - strict persistent ties:
    - 522 of 2,663 retained inventors: 19.6%;
    - 74 nominal deals;
    - mean 3.39 strict anchor partners;
    - 31.0% of strict ties recur by \(+5\).
  - exploratory weak one-patent ties:
    - 1,557 inventors: 58.5%;
    - 132 nominal deals;
    - mean 7.88 anchor partners;
    - 16.4% recur by \(+5\).
- Interpretation:
  - established target-team presence declines strongly among observed patent
    teams;
  - the replacement is mainly collaborators new to both groups and,
    increasingly, outside-group collaboration;
  - very little of observed team composition comes from inventors already
    attached to the acquirer before the deal;
  - strong repeated ties recur more often than the much broader set of
    one-off baseline ties;
  - evidence points to team rebuilding rather than simple absorption into
    pre-existing acquirer teams.
- Required caveats:
  - no untreated comparison and no causal inference;
  - team composition exists only for focal-inventor-years with a co-invented
    patent;
  - composition-defined focal inventors fall from 1,426 at \(+1\) to 620 at
    \(+5\), so later-year composition is selected on continued collaborative
    patenting;
  - patent co-invention is a proxy for codified collaboration, not the full
    knowledge network or employment relationship;
  - annual timing prevents within-year sequencing;
  - `legacy target` and `legacy acquirer` mean prior patent-group affiliation,
    not verified employment.
- Main exhibit:
  - one team-composition figure with the four annual shares.
- Appendix exhibits:
  - strict N0 support/Path Q table;
  - strict-versus-weak tie coverage and cumulative recurrence table;
  - denominator and unresolved-link audit.

## 6.6 Synthesis and limits (0.4--0.6 page)

- Core retained-inventor conclusion:
  - initial inside-group patenting does not imply preserved inventive output;
  - annual patent output is lower by about 0.11 patents;
  - most of the accounting decline occurs during active patenting years;
  - focal patent affiliation is rarely uninterrupted over five years.
- Network contribution:
  - initial target teams are progressively replaced;
  - replacement is not primarily integration into legacy acquirer teams;
  - new and outside collaborators account for the expanding share.
- Identification boundary:
  - quantity result is a selected-group balanced contrast;
  - persistence and N4 network results are descriptive;
  - strict causal network design remains closed on coverage.
- Policy implication:
  - headcount or one early inside-group patent is an insufficient proxy for
    preservation of inventive capacity;
  - durable team continuity and inventor output should be assessed separately.

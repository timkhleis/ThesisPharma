# Pre-outcome amendment: reduced pooled firm basis

No post-treatment outcome has been inspected under the early-recruitment
diagnostic.

The pooled solve that preserved every within-cohort inventor moment and added
all three raw firm moments was infeasible. The two firm-size measures are
highly redundant: log five-year patent stock and log five-year inventor count.

The final pre-outcome firm basis is:

1. a fixed equal-loading size factor, formed as the average of the pooled
   standardized log patent-stock and log inventor-count measures; and
2. firm patent trajectory.

All cohort masses and all within-cohort inventor trajectory constraints remain
unchanged. The original three firm-variable differences are reported
diagnostically. This implements the previously discussed reduced firm
specification (one firm-size factor plus patent intensity/trajectory) without
using outcome information.

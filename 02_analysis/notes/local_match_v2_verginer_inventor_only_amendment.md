# Pre-outcome amendment: inventor-level Verginer solve

No post-treatment outcome has been inspected under the early-recruitment
diagnostic.

The full count-plus-active solve and the count-only solve were both feasible
in 13 of 16 cohorts and both failed in the same cohorts: 1995, 1998, and 1999.
Removing the active indicators therefore did not affect the binding support
problem. The failed cohorts contain only 4, 2, and 9 retained treated deals,
respectively. Requiring these small established-inventor cohorts to reproduce
three cohort-wide firm moments exactly is the binding constraint.

The diagnostic is amended to the inventor-level Verginer specification:

- retain the certified P5 firm-support and technology/caliper restrictions;
- retain early recruitment at event year -7 or -6;
- retain the three-control/two-firm deal gate;
- exactly balance all five annual patent counts, all five annual
  active-patenting indicators, career age, and focal-group exclusivity;
- do not force the three firm variables into the entropy solve;
- report the three firm-variable differences as unconstrained diagnostics;
- apply this same rule to every 1995--2010 cohort.

This changes only the moment constraints, not donor admissibility. It is
appropriate for the stated diagnostic because the question is whether fixed
inventor recruitment and inventor lifecycle account for the patent-count ATT.
The firm layer remains controlled by the already-certified support screen, but
firm means are not claimed to be exactly balanced in this diagnostic.

First Results: Acquisitions and Inventor Productivity

1 July 2026
Preliminary descriptive evidence for supervisor discussion. The causal estimation panel is complete and the did package is installed; no CS(2021) coefficient is reported until the att_gt() run completes and its diagnostics are checked.
Three preliminary facts.
  • The corrected sample is anchored to the published 513-merger universe. It retains 478 uniquely matched target-spine deals after Cassi's target-side exclusions; 345 lie in the 1993-2010 clean window.
  • Among 28,279 pre-deal target inventors in the clean sample, mean annual patenting falls from 0.474 in year -1 to 0.246 in the deal year and 0.113 by year +5. These are descriptive means, not treatment effects.
  • The decline is almost entirely an extensive-margin story: the share of the cohort filing any patent falls from 27.5% (year −1) to 4.7% (year +5), while patents per active inventor actually rises (1.72 → 2.42 over the same window). See Figure 2.

Why the deal count changed

The group-history files cover more mergers than the published sample. The previous reconstruction inadvertently admitted those additional events. The corrected code first anchors each event to deal_map.deal_id, excludes 22 unmatched deals and one ambiguous match, and then applies Cassi's drop flags.

Causal estimation status. The balanced CS(2021) panel passes uniqueness and balance checks, and the official did package now installs and loads cleanly. The att_gt() estimation run (not-yet-treated controls, deal-level clustering) is queued next; no CS(2021) coefficient is reported in this memo until that run completes and passes the pre-trend and cohort-support checks.
